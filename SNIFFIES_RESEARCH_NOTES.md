# Sniffies Feature Research

## Core Data Points
- **Distance**: Precise distance (e.g., "350 ft", "1.2 mi").
- **Last Seen**: Relative time (e.g., "Online Now", "5m ago", "2h ago").
- **Positions**: Roles like "Top", "Bottom", "Vers", "Side", "Strict Top", "Strict Bottom".
- **Looking For**: Tags like "Right Now", "Friends", "Dates", "NSA".
- **Stats**: Age, Height, Weight, Body Type.

## Map Features
- **High-Density Heatmaps**: Clusters of "noses" (profiles) in specific areas.
- **Active Hotspots**: Public cruising areas or busy zones marked with special icons.
- **Live Movement**: Mocked/Real-time updates of user positions.

## Chat & Social
- **Saved Profiles**: "Favorites" or "Starred" list.
- **Media**: Photo/Video sharing with expiration (Snap-style) or permanent.
- **Plans**: Setting a time/location.

## AI Opportunities
- **Plan Guard**: AI monitors chat for "Where", "When", "Let's meet". Notifies user with a distinctive sound before confirming.
- **Style Mimicry**: matching the abrupt/direct style of Sniffies users.

## Brand Identity
- **Logo**: Stylized "Nose" or "Dog" icon.
- **Primary Color**: #00BCD4 (Teal/Cyan) or #00ACC1.
- **Theme**: High-contrast black/dark mode.
